🥇 Mistral drops open-weight model matching GPT-4o performance

Mistral AI released Mixtral-8x22B, an open-weight model that benchmarks within 2% of GPT-4o — The model uses a sparse mixture-of-experts architecture and is licensed for commercial use.

Why it matters: Open-weight models are now competitive with closed APIs, shifting the power balance between startups and incumbents.

Nvidia announces Blackwell Ultra with 5x power efficiency

Nvidia unveiled the Blackwell Ultra chip, promising 5x better performance-per-watt than H100 — The chip targets data centers running multi-trillion-parameter models and is slated for 2027 release.

Why it matters: Hardware efficiency gains are slowing, making software optimizations the next bottleneck.

EU proposes “AI Safety Act” with mandatory red-teaming

The European Commission published a draft AI Safety Act requiring red-teaming for high-risk systems — The rules would apply to models above 10^25 FLOPs and mandate third-party audits.

Why it matters: Regulatory divergence between EU and US could fragment global AI development.

Anthropic open-sources eval suite for agentic workflows

Anthropic released “AgentEval,” a benchmarking toolkit for testing autonomous AI agents — The suite includes 50+ real-world tasks like multi-step research and tool use.

Why it matters: Agentic systems are the next frontier, but reliable evaluation remains unsolved.
