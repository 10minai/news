🥇 U.S. court rules Pentagon’s blacklisting of Anthropic was unlawful

A federal judge called the Defense Department’s move to label Anthropic a supply-chain risk “illegal and baseless” — ending a two-year ban on government contracts.

Why it matters: The ruling sets a precedent for how agencies can (or can’t) exclude AI firms on national security grounds.

Always-on and self-starting AI agents might be OpenAI’s next big play

OpenAI is reportedly developing agents that run continuously and initiate tasks without user prompts — a shift from reactive chatbots to proactive assistants.

Why it matters: If deployed, these agents could redefine productivity tools but also raise new privacy and control concerns.

Meta executive leaves for OpenAI as scrutiny in India grows

Sandhya Devanathan, Meta’s former India head, joins OpenAI to lead Southeast Asia and Australia operations — just as Meta faces regulatory heat in the region.

Why it matters: The move signals OpenAI’s push into high-growth markets while Meta’s local challenges mount.

Beatport blocks fully AI-generated music from its DJ marketplace

The electronic music platform now flags and removes tracks created entirely by AI — citing quality and authenticity concerns.

Why it matters: The decision reflects growing industry backlash against AI-generated content flooding creative markets.

OpenAI backs Thailand’s next generation of AI startups

OpenAI and Thailand’s MHESI launch an eight-week accelerator for ten startups in health, wellness, and education — helping them turn prototypes into market-ready products.
