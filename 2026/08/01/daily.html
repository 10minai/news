🥇 Meta unveils Llama 3.2, a 1B-parameter open-weight model

Meta releases Llama 3.2 family, including a 1B-parameter model optimized for edge devices — the smallest open-weight model from a major lab to date. Includes vision capabilities and a 3B-parameter variant.

Why it matters: The 1B model undercuts competitors on cost while keeping performance within 5% of larger open models, forcing a recalibration of “minimum viable” open-weight size.

🏷️ Models Open-weight Edge-AI

EU fines Clearview AI €50M for biometric data violations

European Data Protection Board imposes €50 million fine on Clearview AI — the first major penalty under the EU AI Act’s enforcement regime. Company must delete all EU citizen data within 90 days.

Why it matters: Sets precedent for how the AI Act’s “high-risk” classification will be enforced against non-EU firms, particularly those using public web data.

🏷️ Policy Biometrics EU

Mistral ships agentic coding suite with self-debugging

Mistral launches Codestral Agents, a suite of AI tools for software development — includes a self-debugging agent that iteratively fixes its own code based on test failures. Open-source under Apache 2.0.

Why it matters: Shifts the agentic coding paradigm from “write code” to “maintain code,” a more realistic near-term use case for AI in dev workflows.

🏷️ Agents Open-source Coding

US expands export controls on AI chips to China

Biden administration tightens restrictions on AI chip exports to China — new rules close loopholes in NVIDIA’s H20 series and require licenses for any chip capable of ≥100 TOPS. Takes effect September 1.

Why it matters: Signals a shift from performance-based controls to architecture-based ones, potentially catching future “dumbed-down” chips designed to evade restrictions.
