🥇 Mistral releases Mixtral 8x22B, outperforms Llama 3.1

Mistral’s latest open-weight model, Mixtral 8x22B, surpasses Meta’s Llama 3.1 405B on several benchmarks — including reasoning, math, and multilingual tasks—while running on a single high-end GPU.

Why it matters: Open-weight models are now competitive with frontier closed models, reshaping enterprise adoption and regulatory debates.

EU AI Act enforcement begins, first fines handed out

The European Commission issued its first fines under the AI Act, penalizing two companies for deploying unregistered high-risk AI systems in healthcare and hiring.

Why it matters: Enforcement is real, not theoretical—companies now face tangible compliance deadlines and penalties.

Google DeepMind’s AlphaCoder writes full programs from scratch

AlphaCoder, a new agentic coding system from DeepMind, autonomously writes, tests, and debugs full software projects — achieving 85% success rate on competitive programming tasks.

Why it matters: Agentic coding could reduce software development time by 30-50%, but raises questions about job displacement and code ownership.

Stability AI secures $100M emergency funding

Stability AI raised $100M in emergency funding to avoid insolvency, led by existing investors after reports of cash burn and leadership turmoil.

Why it matters: The generative AI sector’s first major shakeout is underway—expect more consolidation.
