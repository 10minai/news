🥇 Meta’s Llama 405B overtakes closed models in key benchmarks

Meta releases Llama 405B, the largest open-weight model to date, claiming it outperforms GPT-4 and Claude 3.5 in reasoning and coding tasks. The model is available for research and commercial use under a permissive license.

Why it matters: Open-weight models are now competitive with proprietary ones, shifting the balance of power in AI development.

Nvidia announces Blackwell Ultra amid supply constraints

Nvidia unveils the Blackwell Ultra chip, promising 2x performance over the current H100. The announcement comes as cloud providers report months-long waitlists for AI hardware.

Why it matters: The hardware bottleneck is forcing startups to ration compute or seek alternatives.

EU proposes strict liability rules for high-risk AI

The European Commission drafts AI liability rules, requiring companies to prove their systems didn’t cause harm in high-risk domains like healthcare and law enforcement. Fines could reach 4% of global revenue.

Why it matters: The first major attempt to codify legal responsibility for AI failures.

🏷️ Policy EU Regulation Liability

Stability AI launches text-to-3D model

Stability AI releases Stable 3D, a model that generates 3D assets from text prompts in under 30 seconds. The tool targets game developers and designers.

Why it matters: Accelerates content creation pipelines, reducing reliance on manual 3D modeling.
