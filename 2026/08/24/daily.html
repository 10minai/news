🥇 Mistral drops open-weights model matching GPT-4o

Mistral releases Mixtral-8x22B-Instruct-v0.3, an open-weights model outperforming GPT-4o on six of eight benchmarks — The model is licensed for commercial use and includes a 64k-token context window.

Why it matters: Open-weight models are now competitive with closed APIs, forcing incumbents to rethink pricing and release strategies.

EU AI Act enforcement begins with first fines

The European Commission fines two companies €12M and €8M for deploying unregistered high-risk AI systems — Fines target a facial-recognition vendor and an automated hiring platform.

Why it matters: The first penalties signal regulators won’t tolerate grace-period delays, even for startups.

Google DeepMind trains 100B-parameter agent in simulation

DeepMind’s SIMA agent learns 1,500 skills in a 3D physics sandbox, scaling to 100B parameters — The agent uses a single policy across multiple environments without fine-tuning.

Why it matters: Scaling agents in simulation could unlock generalist robots faster than real-world training.

🏷️ Agents Research Scaling Robotics

US Senate drafts AI liability shield for startups

A bipartisan Senate group proposes a two-year liability shield for AI startups under $50M in revenue — The draft aims to reduce lawsuits while standards are still evolving.

Why it matters: A temporary shield could accelerate startup formation but risks entrenching early movers.
